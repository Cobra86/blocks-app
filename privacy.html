Just Blocks

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026

Just Blocks is a free puzzle game. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your gameplay or any other information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To work, the game saves a few things on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • your best scores, lifetime stats and unlocked achievements;
  • the game you're in the middle of, so you can continue it;
  • your sound and haptics settings.

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can erase it at any time with Settings › Reset all progress, or by deleting the app. If you use iCloud or device backups, Apple may include it in your backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share after a game, iOS opens its standard share sheet with a short text containing your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it, and the app never sees where it goes.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that uses data, such as optional online leaderboards, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
