Common questions

How do I play?
Drag pieces onto the 9×9 board. Fill a row, a column or one of the nine 3×3 boxes to clear it. The game ends when none of your pieces fit. The full rules are under How to Play on the main menu.

Why did my combo stop?
A combo survives two drops that clear nothing. The dots next to the flame show how many you have left.

Can I undo a move?
Yes, your last move, for up to 20 points. The game tells you the price first.

How do I reset my scores?
Settings › Reset all progress. This can't be undone.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Scores, stats and achievements are kept only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them.
